CrossPoint Sleep Screens

Turn an Are.na channel into sleep screens for your Xteink reader. Everything happens in this browser tab.

Screen
Images
Access token

Sent only to api.are.na.

Save to SD card

Put the reader's SD card in your computer, click, and choose the card. On an X4 Pro you can use USB Drive mode instead.

Download ZIP

Open the ZIP and drag the sleep folder onto your SD card.

Send over Wi-Fi Experimental

Chrome only. On the reader, open File Transfer and join your network, then send. Chrome will ask to allow local network access.

Where the files go

SD card
└── sleep
    ├── arena_01.bmp
    ├── arena_02.bmp
    └── …
  1. The sleep folder sits at the top of the card, next to your books, not inside another folder.
  2. On the reader, set Settings → Display → Sleep Screen to Custom.
  3. A file named sleep.bmp at the top of the card replaces these, so remove it if you have one.

Paste a channel above. You'll see every image as it will look on the reader, and can drop any you don't want before saving.
